# 📞 Contact

**Welcome to Our Contact Page**

Thank you for choosing to reach out to us! We're excited to assist you with any questions, concerns, or inquiries you might have. To provide you with the best support experience, we've streamlined our contact process through Discord.

## How to Contact Us:

1. **Open a Ticket:** To ensure prompt and personalized assistance, we kindly request that you open a support ticket. Follow the steps below to get started:

   a. Join our official Discord server: [JOIN](https://discord.com/invite/NCvpxqd4C7) (see [Discord Links](/general/discord-links))

   b. Navigate to the "support" section.

   c. Look for the designated command to initiate a new ticket. (You need to select at least a category for your ticket.)

2. **Provide Details:** Once you've opened a ticket, our team will be notified, and one of our dedicated support members will be assigned to your case. Please provide as much detail as possible about your query or issue.

3. **Stay Updated:** You'll receive real-time notifications on the progress of your ticket. Our team will work diligently to address your concerns and provide solutions.

## Why Discord:

We've chosen Discord as our primary communication platform for its efficiency, reliability, and user-friendly interface. By utilizing tickets, we're able to manage inquiries effectively, prioritize urgent matters, and maintain a record of all communications for reference.

Please note that our support team operates **Monday to Sunday: 7:00 AM CET - 11:00 PM CET**. While we strive to respond as quickly as possible, response times may vary based on the volume of inquiries.

Thank you for considering us for your support needs. We look forward to assisting you on Discord!

Your satisfaction is our priority!

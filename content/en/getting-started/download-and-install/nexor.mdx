# 🤖 Nexor

## Windows

1. Login to your Account on the Nexor Website
2. Move to the "Dashboard" Tab
3. Click on "Download for Windows"
4. Paste the downloaded file into your NilName "scripts" folder

## MacOS

1. Login to your Account on the Nexor Website
2. Move to the "Dashboard" Tab
3. Click on "Download for MacOS"
4. Paste the downloaded file into your Tinkr "scripts" folder

## 4.) Start the applications!

<Callout type="error">
  **Important!**

  The applications (.exe's) can be renamed! In this case they are, so you might not recognize their names.
</Callout>

Ensure that you have both "Nav.exe" and "tool.exe" applications available. In case you find only one or none, it's possible that your antivirus removed them as false positives.

To resolve this, add exceptions or whitelist these applications in your antivirus, or temporarily disable the antivirus during this process.

Start by launching `NoNameNavigation_RenameMe.exe` and then proceed to open `NnLoader_V1.8a.exe`. Once you've completed these steps, you can open your World of Warcraft and enjoy a seamless experience.

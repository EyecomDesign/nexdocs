# 🛒 Buy Nexor

## How to buy Nexor token or session

**1.)** Go to the Nexor website

**2.)** Login with your Discord credentials

**3.)** Buy Nexor Coins (navigate to "Coin Market" tab)

**4.)** Navigate to "Bot" tab

**5.)** Choose your Plan

## Add a session to your Nexor Account

You have the option to enhance each license with an extra session. Any Rotations, profiles or other Premium features reserved for the license are likewise accessible during your session. These sessions can be utilized on different computers, irrespective of the license.

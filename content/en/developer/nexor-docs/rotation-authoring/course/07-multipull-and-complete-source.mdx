# 7. Multipull and complete source

The tutorial has an explicit `MultiPullPulse`. It selects at most three active
enemies, applies only the available instant DoTs, and then reports that the work
is done. The normal engine still owns navigation and combat state.

The gather transition is edge-triggered:

1. Stop the pet and set it passive once when gathering starts.
2. Select no more than three active enemies.
3. Use `Corruption:Cast(enemy)` and `CurseOfAgony:Cast(enemy)` when their own DoT
   is absent. `Cast(target)` keeps the full spell gate.
4. Restore the pet to defensive when no selected target needs work. The next
   combat pulse uses `NX.Pet.Assist()` again.

The multipull path does not add a hardcast to every target, redesign mana policy,
or tell movement how to enter wand range. Optional and talent spells are guarded
by their handles and the normal execute gate.

# Introduction

### What is the Development Area?

The Development Area on the NEXOR platform is a dedicated space designed for approved third-party developers to create, contribute, and manage bot-related projects. It serves as a collaborative environment where developers can leverage platform tools, access exclusive APIs, and collaborate with other community members.

#### Benefits:

- **Exclusive Access:** Gain unparalleled access to developer tools and resources only available to approved partners.
- **Collaboration Opportunities:** Work alongside other developers and the NEXOR Team to enhance the platform's ecosystem.
- **Recognition:** Gain recognition as a trusted developer in the NEXOR community.
- **Monetization Tools:** Use the built-in payment and monetization features to focus on coding rather than finances.
- **Analytics:** Access revenue reports, user analytics, and other essential metrics to refine your strategy.
- **Developer Licenses:** Receive potential complimentary developer licenses to support your work.

### Developer Access Requirements:

To gain access to the official third-party developer area, you need to be an approved developer partner. Access is granted manually by the NEXOR Team based on the following criteria:

- **Registered:** You must be registered on our NEXOR platform.
- **Trusted:** You must be a trusted member of the botting community or earn our trust.
- **GitHub Usage:** All third-party code contributions must be managed via GitHub repositories.
- **Agreement on Payment:** Developers must accept the terms and conditions regarding payment, including potential revenue sharing or subscription models.
- **Lua:** Some level of experience in Lua 5.1 is required, as it's the core scripting language for our platform.
- **WoW API:** Knowledge of the World of Warcraft API is highly beneficial.
- **Commitment to Updates:** Developers should commit to maintaining and updating their code regularly.
- **Open to Feedback:** Developers should be receptive to feedback from both users and the NEXOR Team.

# 19. Add class-specific pet policy

The Warlock rotation chooses when Health Funnel may spend the player's health.
Use the current option values and the refreshing unit locals:

```lua
HealthFunnel:CastableIf(function()
    return NX.Toggles["Pet"]
        and NX.Pet.IsPetActive()
        and Pet:GetHP() < NX.Options["Health Funnel Pet Health Percent"]
        and Player:GetHP() > NX.Options["Minimum Health Percent"]
end):SetTarget(Pet)
```

Put the two sliders inside the Mana and health section, with defaults 50 and 35,
respectively. The framework returns the declared defaults and numeric health;
`or 100` and fallback thresholds would hide authoring mistakes.

# 14. Attach spell policy

Attach a predicate once near the top of the file. It expresses rotation intent;
it should not repeat mechanical spell gates:

```lua
Corruption:CastableIf(function()
    local target = Target()
    return NX.Toggles["Damage"] ~= false
        and target:IsEnemy()
        and not target:HasMyDebuff(Corruption)
end)
```

The predicate answers whether the rotation wants the spell. `Execute()` later
checks known-spell state, cooldown/GCD, range, line of sight, facing, movement,
cast state, usability, and target validity.

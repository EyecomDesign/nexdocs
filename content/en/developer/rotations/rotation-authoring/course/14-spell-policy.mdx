# 14. Attach spell policy

Write one condition directly on each catalog spell in Spell conditions. This
single-target version expresses damage policy and the missing DoT:

```lua
Corruption:CastableIf(function()
    return NX.Toggles["Damage"] and not Target:HasMyDebuff(Corruption)
end)
```

`Execute()` handles known-spell state, cooldown/GCD, range, line of sight,
facing, movement, casting, usability and target kind. Do not repeat an enemy or
friend gate in the predicate. A custom selector can still use `IsEnemy()` to
choose a unit; that is selection, not a duplicate cast gate.

A second `CastableIf` overwrites the first. Write each spell's policy once,
without an `AttachDot` helper. The bounded selectors in lesson 23 move DoT-target
selection out of this condition and preserve Corruption's live cast-time refresh.

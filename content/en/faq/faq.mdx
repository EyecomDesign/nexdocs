To enhance your leveling experience and increase your XP/H, consider the following tips:

1. Upgrade your gear to ensure you're equipped with the best possible items for your level.
2. Keep your spells up to date by regularly upgrading them.
3. Purchase health and mana potions and remember to enable their usage in your INVENTORY settings.

6. Keep an eye on your bot's survivability. Ensure it's not dying too often.

<details>
<summary>Why is my browser / antivirus blocking the download / files?</summary>

Game hacks have earned a reputation for triggering false positives on antivirus software. This is primarily due to the resemblance in behavior between malware and injectors, leading the antivirus to mistakenly flag them as potential threats.

To mitigate such issues, we highly recommend utilizing a separate machine solely dedicated to botting. On this dedicated device, it is advisable to disable the antivirus temporarily to prevent any interference with the bot's operations.

However, if you need to run the bot on your main machine, we strongly recommend creating exception rules within the antivirus settings, allowing all processes and files within the unlocker folder to run uninterrupted.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My bot is not moving / just unstuck. What to do?</summary>

Ensure you've correctly set the file paths for your mmaps files. If you haven't downloaded the mesh files yet, please refer to the [Windows installation guide](/getting-started/download-and-install/noname-windows) again.

For Classic, TBC, and WotLK versions, the files should be located in:
**nn/mmaps/wotlk/FilesHere**

For the Retail version, files should be in:
**nn/mmaps/sl/FilesHere**

It's crucial that there are no extra folders present. A frequent error is having nested folders, such as mmaps/wotlk/wotlk, which will cause issues.

Additionally, your nav.exe must be operational. If it shuts down unexpectedly, you may need to install or update your C++ redistributables and/or your .NET framework.

</details>

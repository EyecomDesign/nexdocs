# Buying, repair and reusable settings

Open **Settings → Buying**. Opening the page shows a preview; it does not purchase items. **Automatic buying** controls ordinary trips. **Restock now** requests a real visit and can move the character, even when automatic buying is off. Category switches, Never buy rules and the money reserve still apply.

## Configure a supply target

1. In **Food** or **Drinks**, enable the category, then set **Restock to** and **Start trip at**. A target of zero disables buying that category; the trip threshold is kept below the target.
2. For example, choose Restock to 20 and Start trip at 5. At five remaining or fewer, an automatic trip can be requested. A useful vendor encountered on another service trip can top up before that point.
3. Review **Money reserve** in gold, silver and copper. Buying stops before spending below that floor; the default is zero. Prices and availability are confirmed at the vendor.
4. Check **Reagents & custom items** and **Never buy**. The latter can exclude an otherwise eligible item. Pet-food buying requires accepted food for the active pet; ammunition settings depend on the character and weapon.
5. Leave **Use the profile's vendor** off for automatic selection, or enable it when your loaded profile contains a suitable service NPC. A recorded NPC still needs an accessible route and the required stock.

Food, drink and pet-food targets default to 20, with a trip threshold of zero. Food/drink tier selection defaults to the second-best usable vendor tier. Saved settings can differ. [Recovery](/en/using-nexor/recovery) explains how the active catalog classifies supplies already in your bags.

## Run and check one visit

With room in your bags and enough money above the reserve, request **Restock now**. Watch the buying state and bag quantities. **Last restock** and the supply-row reasons help distinguish purchased, already stocked, excluded and unavailable supplies. A request is not proof that the desired item was sold by that merchant.

If nothing is bought, check the enabled category, target, Never buy list, reserve, bag space, usable item level and merchant stock. Missing vendors/routes and unavailable items cannot be fixed by raising the target. Keep a small manual supply while investigating; avoid repeatedly forcing the same failed trip.

Smart Town can group useful service stops around an automatic visit and resumes ordinary work afterward. It compares available services and routes; it cannot guarantee a vendor or route that is absent from the current profile/world data. Buying, selling, repairing and mailing retain their own transaction rules.

## Repair

Open **Settings → Vendor Management**. Enable **Auto Repair**, select **Repair on Lowest Item Durability** or **Repair on Average Durability**, and set **Repair Percentage**. **Use Profile NPC** chooses profile repair data; **Trigger Repair** requests a visit immediately. Leave **Ignore Combat while Repairing** off unless you deliberately want that behavior.

For example, set a repair threshold suitable for your run, damage an expendable piece through ordinary play, and observe a repair visit when the chosen durability measure is below the threshold. Confirm durability increases and check the money spent. A merchant without repair service or insufficient funds can prevent completion.

Review [Sell, Keep and Delete rules](/en/using-nexor/item-rules) before combining a supply visit with a sale.

## Save the settings for reuse

Open **Home → Import-Export Settings**:

- **Save New** asks for a name and saves the current settings, for example `Safe supply setup`.
- Select an existing entry and choose **Overwrite** to replace that preset with the current settings.
- Select an entry and choose **Load** to apply it. Reopen the settings pages and confirm the intended values before starting.

Presets live under `scripts/Nexor_User/Settings/Saved Settings`. They store application settings; a world profile separately describes locations, units and services. Loading one is not a substitute for loading the other.

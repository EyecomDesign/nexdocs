# 🤖 Nexor

Follow the steps for your operating system.

## Windows

1. Sign in to your account on the Nexor website.
2. Open the **Dashboard** tab.
3. Click **Download for Windows**.
4. Copy the downloaded file to the NilName `scripts` folder.

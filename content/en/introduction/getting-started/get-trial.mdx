# 🧪 Get Trial

Open the [Nexor website](https://nexor.app/) and check the trial offer currently
available to your signed-in account. Before starting it, review its eligibility,
duration, included products, and license terms. Trial availability and terms need
current confirmation; this guide does not establish them from source code.

Confirm the exact supported game client/build and unlocker requirements first.
The unlocker's own purchase or trial terms are separate; check them with its
vendor. If the website does not show a trial or the terms are unclear, contact
[official Nexor support](/en/introduction#discord-links).

After obtaining access, follow [installation](/en/introduction/getting-started/download-and-install)
and [Account and Sessions](/en/introduction/getting-started/account-and-sessions).

## What is Nexor? [#what-is-nexor]

Nexor automates common World of Warcraft tasks so you can spend less time on repetitive work. It supports:

- Grinding, questing, and gathering, including navigation, resource collection, and quest objectives.
- Fishing, battlegrounds, and dungeons.
- Buying food and drink, equipping gear, repairing equipment, using flight masters, crafting bandages, and disenchanting items.
- Blacklisting, unstucking, and Rotation Mode.
- Adjustable class rotations and custom profiles.
- Mailing, Hearthstone binding and use, and travel actions.
- Weekly updates.

For help, open a ticket in the [Nexor Discord server](#discord-links).

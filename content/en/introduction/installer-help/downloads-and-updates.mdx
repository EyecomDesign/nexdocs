# 🌐 Downloads and Updates

The installer downloads the Nexor loader, your Key file, the assets and its own updates from the
Nexor servers. Every downloaded file is checked before it is used.

## "Nexor could not reach the download server"

The installer tried a few times. Check your internet connection, then try again. A VPN, proxy or
firewall can also block the download.

## "Could not reach the Nexor website" or "The Nexor website is busy"

Check your internet connection. The status pill in the title bar shows whether the Nexor
services run. Wait a few minutes and try again.

## "Could not download …"

The server answered, but the file was not there. Trying again right away does not help. Wait a
while, or open a ticket in the Nexor Discord.

## "does not match its published fingerprint" or "is not valid"

A downloaded file, or the file that describes it, did not pass the check. Nothing was changed.
Try again later. If it keeps happening, your network or antivirus may change downloads.

## "Update the Nexor Installer first"

A newer installer is ready. Press the Main button (it says **Update**). The installer downloads
the update, checks it and restarts. Until then, Play and setup changes wait.

## "Setup is still working. Update the installer when it finishes."

Wait until setup finishes, then press **Update** again.

## "Could not update the installer"

Try again later. If it keeps failing, download the newest installer from the
[Download page](https://nexorapp.azurewebsites.net/download) and run it. Your settings stay.

# 🔓 NoName Not Found

Nexor runs inside the NoName unlocker (also called NilName). The installer needs your NoName
folder to put the Nexor loader and your Key file there.

## What a NoName folder looks like

A NoName folder holds a `scripts` folder and a `license.txt` file. It usually also holds `lua`
and `load_glue` folders and a program that ends in `.exe`.

## The installer found no NoName folder

- **You do not have NoName yet.** In Setup, click **Get NoName**. Your browser downloads the
  official `NoName.zip`. The installer finds the download in your Downloads folder and unpacks it
  into a new, empty folder for you.
- **NoName is somewhere the search does not look.** Choose the folder yourself. Pick the folder
  that holds `scripts` and `license.txt`, not the `scripts` folder itself.
- **"is not a NoName folder"**: the folder you picked misses `scripts` or `license.txt`. Pick
  the folder one level up or down, or download NoName again.
- **"Choose your NoName folder first"**: finish the first Setup step before the next one.

## Unpacking the NoName zip did not work

- **"This zip does not hold a NoName folder"**: the file is not the official NoName download.
  Delete it and click **Get NoName** again.
- **"The NoName zip was refused"** or **"Could not read"**: the download is broken or still
  running. Wait until the browser has finished, or download it again.
- **"already holds files"**: choose an empty or new folder for NoName.
- **"is not a full folder path"**: choose the folder with the folder picker.

## The license key

NoName needs your license key in `license.txt`. In Setup (or **Settings → Advanced**), paste the
key you got when you bought NoName. If the installer says the key does not look right, copy it
again from your NoName purchase, without extra spaces or lines. The installer writes the key only
into `license.txt` and keeps no copy.

## The NoName program

Play starts the NoName program you chose. If the installer asks you to choose it, open
**Settings → Advanced** and pick the `.exe` in your NoName folder.

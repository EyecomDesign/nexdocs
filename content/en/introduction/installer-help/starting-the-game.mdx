# ▶️ Starting NoName

**Play** in the sidebar starts NoName and closes the installer. NoName then starts WoW.

## "Setup is still working"

Wait until the installer finishes what it is doing, then press Play again.

## The installer says a game or NoName is running

Play does not start a second copy. Close WoW and NoName (the message names what runs), then press
Play again.

## "Choose your NoName program first" or "is not a program in your NoName folder"

Open **Settings → Advanced** and pick the NoName program (the `.exe`) in your NoName folder.

## "Could not start …"

- Windows or your antivirus may have blocked NoName. Allow it, then try again.
- If Windows asked for admin rights and you clicked **No**, press Play again and click **Yes**.
- If the NoName program was moved or deleted, choose it again in **Settings → Advanced**.

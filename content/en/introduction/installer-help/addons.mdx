# 🧩 Addons

The **Addons** page lists the addons Nexor recommends for each of your WoW folders. The installer
installs them into `Interface\AddOns` and remembers which ones it installed. It never touches an
addon folder it did not install.

## "The recommended addons are not available yet" or "is not valid"

The list comes from the Nexor website. Try again later. **"needs a newer installer"** means:
update the installer first.

## "Could not find a download for …"

The addon's download could not be found or reached. Try again later.

## "The download of … was refused"

The downloaded zip did not pass the installer's safety checks (for example, it was too big or held
unsafe paths). Nothing was changed.

## "The addon folder … is already there and was not installed by Nexor"

You already have a folder with that name, installed by you or another tool. Nexor leaves it alone.
To let Nexor install the addon, remove or rename that folder yourself first.

## "The addon folder … belongs to …"

Two recommended addons use the same folder. Remove the other one first.

## "The addon folder … was changed since Nexor installed it"

You changed files in that addon. Nexor will not overwrite your changes. Remove the folder yourself,
or choose **Stop managing** to keep it as it is.

## "not on the recommended list … any more" or "Nexor did not install this addon"

The list changed, or the addon was not installed by Nexor. You can still remove an addon that
Nexor installed.

## "Nexor could not finish the change and could not put everything back"

Something stopped the change half way. Your old addon folders are kept in the folder the message
names. Move them back into the `AddOns` folder yourself.

## "is not one of your confirmed WoW folders for this Flavor"

Confirm that WoW folder in Setup first.

# 🔑 Sign-in Problems

The installer signs you in through the Nexor website. **Sign in** opens the website in your
browser. After you sign in there, the browser sends you back to the installer. The sign-in then
lasts 30 days.

## "Please sign in first" or "Your sign-in has run out"

Click **Sign in** and sign in on the website again.

## "Sign-in did not finish" or "Sign-in was cancelled"

- Finish the sign-in in the browser tab the installer opened. Leave the installer open while you
  do it.
- If you closed the browser tab, or the browser shows nothing, click **Sign in** again. The
  installer starts a new sign-in.
- If the website said your account is blocked, see below.

## "The sign-in answer did not come from this installer"

The browser sent back an answer that belongs to an older sign-in. Nothing was saved. Click
**Sign in** again and use only the newest browser tab.

## "Sign-in did not work"

The website did not accept the sign-in. Click **Sign in** again. If it keeps failing, open a
ticket in the Nexor Discord.

## "Your Nexor account is blocked"

The website refused your account. Open a ticket in the Nexor Discord; the installer cannot change
this.

## "Could not keep your sign-in safe"

The installer keeps your sign-in protected by Windows for your Windows user. If Windows refuses,
sign out of Windows and back in, then try again. If it keeps failing, open a ticket and paste the
support info (**Settings → Advanced**).

## The Nexor website is busy or cannot be reached

See [Downloads and updates](/en/introduction/installer-help/downloads-and-updates). Wait a few
minutes and try again.

## "Signing in through the installer is not available yet"

The website has switched installer sign-in off for now. You can still set up Nexor by hand: see
the last part of [SmartScreen warning](/en/introduction/installer-help/smartscreen).
